CDCU — Central Delivery Coordinating Unit

The Office of the Secretary to the Government of the Federation needed one place to see whether government projects across dozens of agencies were actually getting delivered. I designed a monitoring dashboard that tracks every deliverable nationwide — by region, by agency, by status — replacing scattered reports with one source of truth for completed, ongoing, overdue, and abandoned projects.

Fintech · Web dashboard

CoopPoint — Cooperative Management Platform

Savings cooperatives run on manual registers and WhatsApp updates — members can't see their own standing, and cooperative heads can't see the whole picture at once. I designed CoopPoint to give a cooperative head one dashboard for contributions, savings, loans, and withdrawals, plus a clear approval flow for new member registrations, from application through KYC review to accept/reject.

Solo — client project Design concept
Cooperative overview — contributions, savings, loans, withdrawals.
Registered members — status & savings.
Member review — KYC & decision.
EdTech · Web app + marketing

Adept for Education — School Management System

Schools juggle fee collection, student records, and reporting across spreadsheets and manual bank reconciliation. I designed Adept, a SaaS platform that assigns each student a dedicated bank account, lets parents pay fees with instant receipts, and gives school admins one dashboard for revenue, outstanding payments, and student records — covering the marketing site, auth, dashboard, and core data views.

Solo — client project Design concept
Marketing site — the value proposition, built around the dashboard it sells.
School dashboard — revenue and term progress.
Auth with onboarding context.
Student records, searchable & filterable.
